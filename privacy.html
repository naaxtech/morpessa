In short: Morpessa is a QA Engineer app for Discord. She processes your Discord identifiers and the messages, commands and files you deliberately send to her so she can test Naaxtech projects and report findings. We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use your Discord content to train AI models.

01Who we are

Morpessa ("Morpessa", the "App") is a Discord application developed and operated by NAAX TECHNOLOGIES CORP ("Naaxtech", "we", "us", "our"), a corporation registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of the Republic of the Philippines. Naaxtech is the personal information controller for the processing described in this Policy, except where we process data on behalf of a client under a separate agreement, in which case that client is the controller and we act as its personal information processor.

This Policy is issued in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission ("NPC"), as well as the Discord Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy. Where other privacy laws apply to you (for example, the EU/UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), we honour the rights those laws provide.

02Scope

This Policy applies to information processed when you:

  • add Morpessa to, or interact with Morpessa in, a Discord server, channel, thread or direct message;
  • use Morpessa's slash commands, mentions, buttons, menus or other interactive components;
  • are a member of a server where Morpessa is installed and your messages appear in a channel Morpessa has been granted access to and is asked to act on; and
  • visit this website.

It does not apply to Discord itself. Discord's processing of your data is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy. It also does not apply to third-party websites or applications that Morpessa tests or links to, which have their own policies.

03Information we process

a. Discord account and server data

Provided to us by Discord when you interact with the App:

  • Discord user ID, username, display name, server nickname and avatar URL;
  • server (guild) ID and name, channel and thread IDs and names, and role IDs relevant to permissions;
  • the permissions granted to Morpessa and to the user invoking her, used to decide what she is allowed to do.

b. Content you send to Morpessa

  • slash commands and their options, and messages in which you mention Morpessa or reply to her;
  • where Discord's Message Content access is enabled for the App, the content of messages in channels or threads where Morpessa has been asked to work, limited to what is needed to understand and complete the request and its context;
  • attachments you provide, such as screenshots, logs, recordings or files describing a bug or test scenario.

c. QA work product

  • test plans, test runs, results, issue reports, reproduction steps, screenshots and logs generated while Morpessa tests a Naaxtech project;
  • URLs, environments, builds and test accounts that you instruct Morpessa to use. Do not give Morpessa production credentials or real customers' personal data unless that has been agreed in writing with Naaxtech; Morpessa is intended to test with test accounts and test data.

d. Technical and operational data

  • timestamps, command and interaction IDs, error and diagnostic logs, rate-limit and usage counters, and performance metrics needed to run, secure and debug the App.

e. Website data

This website is static. It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider and the font provider (Google Fonts) may process your IP address and browser information in standard server logs to deliver the page.

04What we do not collect

  • We do not read, store or index the message history of servers in bulk, and we do not process messages in channels Morpessa cannot access.
  • We do not collect your Discord password, email address, phone number or payment information.
  • We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (as defined in the Data Privacy Act, such as health, government identifiers or information about race, religion or political affiliation). Please do not send it to Morpessa.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or to build profiles of individuals.

06Lawful basis for processing

Under Sections 12 and 13 of the Data Privacy Act (and, where applicable, Article 6 of the GDPR), we rely on:

  • Contract: processing necessary to provide the App you or your organisation chose to use;
  • Legitimate interests: operating, securing and improving the App, and maintaining reliable QA records for Naaxtech projects, balanced against your rights and freedoms;
  • Consent: where you voluntarily send content or attachments to Morpessa. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Controls & removal), without affecting processing before withdrawal;
  • Legal obligation: where processing is required by law or lawful order.

07Automated and AI-assisted processing

Morpessa uses automated tools, including third-party large language model ("AI") services, to understand requests, drive test sessions and write reports. Content relevant to a request (for example, your instruction, the page being tested and resulting screenshots or logs) may be sent to an AI provider to generate a response.

  • We use AI providers under commercial or API terms that do not permit them to train their models on our inputs or outputs.
  • We do not use your Discord content to train AI models.
  • Morpessa does not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about individuals. Her reports are reviewed and acted on by people.
  • AI output can be inaccurate. Treat Morpessa's findings as an aid to, not a replacement for, human judgement.

08Sharing and disclosure

We share information only as follows:

  • Within the Discord server. Morpessa's replies and reports are posted to the channel, thread or message where she was invoked and are visible to anyone with access to it.
  • Service providers (processors). Cloud hosting and infrastructure, database and storage, AI model providers, error monitoring and browser/testing infrastructure, each bound by written agreements requiring confidentiality, security and use only on our instructions.
  • Naaxtech personnel and project teams. Authorised Naaxtech staff and contractors working on the relevant project, on a need-to-know basis and under confidentiality obligations.
  • Clients. Where Morpessa tests a project for a Naaxtech client, the QA findings for that project may be shared with that client.
  • Legal and safety. Where required by law, regulation, court order or lawful request of a government authority, or where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of Naaxtech, our users or the public.
  • Business transfers. In a merger, acquisition, reorganisation or sale of assets, subject to this Policy's protections continuing to apply.

09International transfers

Naaxtech is based in the Philippines, and our service providers (including Discord and AI and cloud providers) may store or process data in other countries, such as the United States. When we transfer personal information outside the Philippines, we remain accountable for it under Section 21 of the Data Privacy Act and use contractual and other safeguards to ensure a comparable level of protection.

11Security

We implement organisational, physical and technical measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit, restricted and logged access to systems on a least-privilege basis, secure storage of the App's Discord token and other secrets, separation of test environments from production, and regular review of our providers. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe Morpessa has been misused or compromised, contact us immediately.

12Your rights

As a data subject under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed whether and how your personal information is processed;
  • access your personal information and information about its processing;
  • object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erasure or blocking of data that is unlawfully obtained, no longer necessary, or processed against your rights;
  • data portability, to obtain your data in a structured, commonly used electronic format;
  • damages for harm sustained from inaccurate, incomplete, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of personal data; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

If you are in the EU, UK or another jurisdiction with similar laws, you may also have rights to restrict processing and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. To exercise any right, email us from the details in Contact and include your Discord user ID. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within thirty (30) days or any shorter period required by law. Exercising your rights is free unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

13Controls and removal

  • Stop interacting. Morpessa acts only when invoked. You can simply not mention or command her.
  • Server administrators can restrict Morpessa's channel access and permissions in Discord's server settings, or remove her from the server at any time ("Kick" in the member list or Server Settings → Integrations).
  • Deletion requests. Ask us to delete data associated with your Discord user ID or your server, and we will do so unless we must retain it under law or a client agreement, in which case we will tell you why.

14Children

Morpessa is a professional tool and is not directed at children. Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or older where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Morpessa, contact us and we will delete it.

15Personal data breach

If a personal data breach is likely to give rise to a real risk of serious harm, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and affected data subjects within seventy-two (72) hours of knowledge of the breach, as required by NPC Circular No. 16-03 and its successor issuances, and take steps to contain and remedy it.

16Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to Morpessa, our practices or the law. We will post the updated Policy on this page and change the "Last updated" date. For material changes, we will provide reasonable notice, for example through Morpessa or a notice in servers where she is installed. Continued use of Morpessa after the effective date means the updated Policy applies.
